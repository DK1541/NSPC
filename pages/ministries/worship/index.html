The sacraments

Communion and baptism

Communion

The first Sunday of every month. We practise open communion — anyone who trusts Jesus is welcome at the table, children included.

Baptism

We baptise infants as an acknowledgement of God's lifelong presence, during Sunday worship so the whole congregation shares the promise.
