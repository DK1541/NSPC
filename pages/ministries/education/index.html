Education

Faith is learned at every age

Godly Play, Sunday School, Confirmation, and a Wednesday morning Bible study that has been running a long time.

By age

Where each age group belongs

Godly Play · 2–5 years

Our younger children go to the Godly Play room on the second floor, where teachers supervise play and read a few stories.

Sunday School · grades 1–5

Each month children explore Bible stories through art, service projects and music. On the first Sunday they sing in worship, then go to the library for story time and to borrow books, and return for communion. Every child is assigned a deacon buddy for the year.

Confirmation · grades 6–9

A joint programme with other churches in Milwaukee Presbytery, meeting monthly for study and a meal, hosted by a different church each time. In the second year, youth build a mission immersion project with a mentor.

Adult Bible Study

Wednesdays at 10:30am — biblical texts, church history and theology, with devotionals from A Sanctified Art during Advent and Lent. Every other week in summer.
