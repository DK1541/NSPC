Music

Choir Invitationals

Can't make it to every weekly rehearsal? Join us on a Choir Invitational Sunday. Meet the choir in the sanctuary at 9:15, learn the music, and sing for just one service.

Chancel Choir

Sings during weekly worship and for all special services. Rehearses once a week.

Children's Choir

Children learn music during the last 15 minutes of Sunday School and sing every first Sunday.
