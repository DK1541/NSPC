Mission Partners

Food, shelter, theatre, and a seventy-year friendship

Six organisations we work with rather than write cheques to.

Our partners

Who we work with and what we do together

Food for the Journey

Five churches in Southeast Wisconsin yoked together to garden for neighborhoods living in Milwaukee's food deserts. We keep six raised beds and send the produce to Apostle Presbyterian Food Pantry. food4thejourney.org

Gather and Grow Shared Kitchen

Our own kitchen, being upgraded to commercial grade and rented to local food entrepreneurs across the North Shore.

Milwaukee Youth Theatre

Open auditions for every show regardless of experience, with students working in every part of a production. They have performed here since autumn 2025. milwaukeeyouththeatre.org

Congregation Shalom

A Jewish congregation we have worked alongside for more than seventy years, through the annual Interfaith Thanksgiving service and youth-led service projects. cong-shalom.org

Pathfinders

Supporting homeless youth with resources and advocacy. We host their annual Christmas dinner. pathfindersmke.org

Divine Intervention

Tippecanoe Presbyterian's winter shelter, offering shelter, safety and sustenance from December through March. tippemission.org
