The things people wonder about before a first Sunday

Nobody asks these out loud, so here are the answers in advance.

What do I wear?

Whatever you own. You'll see a suit and you'll see jeans in the same pew, and no one is counting.

How long does it last?

About an hour. Coffee afterward for as long as you like, and no obligation to stay for it.

Where do I park?

On Bartlett or Olive, free on Sundays. The Bartlett entrance is step-free.

What about my kids?

Bring them. Godly Play for ages 2–5, Sunday School for grades 1–5, and nobody minds noise in the meantime.

Do I have to believe it all?

No. This congregation holds a wide range of politics and theology. You are welcome wherever you are on the way.

Can I take communion?

Yes — the first Sunday of every month, and the table is open to anyone who trusts Jesus, children included.

Out in the neighborhood

Most of what we do happens outside the building

A summer camp, an after-school programme, six raised garden beds feeding a food pantry, and a block party that closes the street.

Compassion Camp

Two August weeks for children entering 4K through 5th grade, built on kindness and hands-on service. $60 a week, $10 off each additional child.

Camp details

Kindness World Explorers

After school on Wednesdays for elementary students, exploring kindness and world cultures through art. $20 a month, snack included.

Programme details

Good Neighbour Exchange

Two donation days and a free store day each summer, with Catch the Wave Shorewood and the Shorewood Waters Project. Everything free, nobody asked why they came.

Across the year

Three times a year the church spills onto the street

Easter Egg Hunt

The Saturday before Easter. Crafts, stories, and an egg hunt for every child who turns up.

Block Party

The Saturday before school starts. We close the street for food trucks, ice cream, games and a bounce house.

Interfaith Thanksgiving

Every November with Congregation Shalom, a partnership now in its eighth decade.
