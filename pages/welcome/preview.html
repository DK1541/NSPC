Whatever brought you here — curiosity, a hard season, a child who wants to sing — there is room.

Before you come

What a first Sunday actually looks like

Arrive whenever

People trickle in from about ten to. Nobody will make you stand up and introduce yourself.

Wear what you own

A suit and a pair of jeans sit in the same pew most weeks.

An hour, near enough

Coffee afterwards for as long as you like, and no obligation to stay.

Children stay or go

Godly Play for ages 2–5, Sunday School for grades 1–5, and nobody minds noise.

The table is open

Communion the first Sunday of the month, open to anyone who trusts Jesus, children included.

Parking is free
