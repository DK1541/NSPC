History

A hundred years on the same corner

From a storefront mission with Sunday School in the old village jail, to a centennial in 2022.

Since 1922

How we got here

  1. 1922

    Begins as a mission of Westminster Presbyterian, meeting in a Shorewood storefront. The old village jail beside the village hall serves as the Sunday School room.

  2. 1923

    The congregation formally organizes. The first service of the Shorewood Presbyterian Society is held on 16 June.

  3. 1924

    The first building goes up, a wooden structure on the corner.

  4. 1951

    The name changes to North Shore Presbyterian Church.

  5. 1952

    The colonial brick building is dedicated — the sanctuary we still worship in.

  6. 1959

    The educational wing is added.

  7. 2002

    The memorial patio is built, now home to summer worship at the garden.

  8. 2015

    The pineapple becomes our mark. It is the universal image of hospitality, which is the promise we most wanted to make in public.

  9. 2022

    One hundred years.
