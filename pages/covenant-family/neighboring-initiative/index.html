Neighboring Initiative

Three times a year the church spills onto the street

Not outreach aimed at the neighborhood — events held with it. Free, open, and nobody asked what they believe at the door.

What we run

Held with Shorewood, not at it

Easter Egg Hunt

The Saturday before Easter. Crafts, stories, and an egg hunt for every child who turns up.

Block Party

The Saturday before school starts. We close the street for food trucks, ice cream, games and a bounce house.

Shorewood Spring Clean Out

A free exchange with Shorewood Buy Nothing, in the freecycle spirit. Bring what you don't need, take what you do.

All we're ever asked to do in this life is to treat our neighbor exactly as we would hope to be treated ourselves.
