Staff

Who you'll meet

Rev. Jeannine Oakes Daugherty — Pastor

Ordained a teaching elder in the PC(USA) since May 2015. She studied at UIC and Yale, holds an MA in Urban Ministry from McCormick, and moved to Wisconsin in January 2024 with her wife Denise and their three daughters. jeannine@northshorepres.org

Sean DeBoth — Director of Music

Director of Music here since 2004. He teaches at Lake Denoon Middle School and holds degrees from Carroll College and UWM. nspcmusic@gmail.com

Jeff Peterson — Organist

Professor emeritus at UWM, with degrees from Oberlin and the University of Illinois. He has played for churches for more than four decades. jeffry@uwm.edu

Leanne Hoeg — Office & Financial Assistant

Originally from Mediapolis, Iowa, with more than 25 years in office administration. She is usually the first voice you hear. office@northshorepres.org

Becky Schuppman — Children's Music Director

Becky directs the children's choir, which sings in worship each month and presents the Christmas pageant every December.
