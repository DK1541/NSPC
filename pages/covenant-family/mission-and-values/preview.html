Mission & Values

Wherever you are on your faith journey, you are welcome here

We welcome you with open hearts and minds. This congregation holds a wide range of politics and theology, united by a commitment to follow Jesus.

Creating a Spiritual Home, Growing the Covenant Family.

How we spend what we have

Four things we have promised to do

Worship God

Together, every week, in the sanctuary and out at the garden.

Welcome others

With Christian compassion, whoever they are and whatever brought them.

Promote faith development

Education at every age, from two years old to Wednesday morning Bible study.

Advocate for peace and justice

And address suffering where we find it, starting on our own street.
