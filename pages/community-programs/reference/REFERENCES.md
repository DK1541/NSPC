# References — Community Programs — camp, after school, exchange

**Our niche:** mainline Protestant, neighbourhood scale, one Sunday service, welcoming and
progressive, community-service oriented. Roughly PC(USA), Episcopal, ELCA, UCC.

**Not our niche:** megachurch (Elevation, VOUS, Transformation), contemporary/evangelical
multi-campus. Those dominate almost every "best church website" list — their design
vocabulary is production value and scale. Ours is hospitality and longevity.

Worship Times, cited repeatedly below, builds *only* for mainline denominations, so their
portfolio is the closest peer set available.

## Peer congregations
1. [Memorial Drive Presbyterian — Outreach](https://www.mdpc.org/ministries/outreach/) — a food pantry serving roughly 400 families a week, clearly presented.
2. [Faith Presbyterian Austin — Mission & Outreach](https://faithpresaustin.org/mission-and-outreach/)
3. [Grace Episcopal, Asheville — Outreach](https://www.graceavl.com/outreach/) — housing, food, sanctuary, equity. Closest match to our justice framing.
4. [Pathway Church — Outreach](https://pathway.church/ministries/outreach/) — a pantry garden harvested and delivered weekly. Direct parallel to Food for the Journey.
5. [St. Mary Magdalene, Pittsburgh](https://saintmarymagdalenepgh.org/social-ministries-1) — three pantries plus a community garden.
6. [St. Apostle — Outreach & Social Justice](https://www.stapostle.org/ministries/outreach-social-justice-ministries/) — concrete volunteer asks rather than vague invitations.
7. [Our Lady Queen of Peace — Social Justice](https://www.ourladyqueenofpeace.org/social-justice-and-outreach.html)
8. [Canvas Presbyterian](https://worshiptimes.org/featured-ministry/canvas-presbyterian-church/)
9. [Salem First Presbyterian](https://worshiptimes.org/featured-ministry/salem-first-presbyterian-church/)
10. [Christ Congregational UCC](https://christcongregationalucc.org/)

## What to take
Numbers make outreach real — "400 families a week", "six raised beds". State the specific
volunteer ask and the next date. This is our strongest section on content: Compassion Camp,
Kindness World Explorers and the Exchange all already have real detail.
