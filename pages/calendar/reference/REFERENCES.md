# References — Calendar & Events

**Our niche:** mainline Protestant, neighbourhood scale, one Sunday service, welcoming and
progressive, community-service oriented. Roughly PC(USA), Episcopal, ELCA, UCC.

**Not our niche:** megachurch (Elevation, VOUS, Transformation), contemporary/evangelical
multi-campus. Those dominate almost every "best church website" list — their design
vocabulary is production value and scale. Ours is hospitality and longevity.

Worship Times, cited repeatedly below, builds *only* for mainline denominations, so their
portfolio is the closest peer set available.

## What to take
A raw Google embed looks like a spreadsheet. Put the weekly rhythm in hand-written markup
above it and let the embed carry one-offs. Our annual events — egg hunt, block party, camp,
exchange, interfaith Thanksgiving — deserve cards, not calendar rows.
