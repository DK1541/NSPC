Bring what you don't need. Take what you do.

Good Neighbour Exchange

Two days of donations, then a free store day — everything at no cost, for anyone who needs it. A partnership between North Shore Presbyterian Church, Catch the Wave Shorewood, and the Shorewood Waters Project.

Next exchange: dates to be announced

We hold the Exchange each summer at 4048 N. Bartlett Ave., Shorewood. Check back here or contact the office at office@northshorepres.org to hear when the next one is set.

What we can take

Gently used and in working order, please — if you'd give it to a friend, we'd love to have it.

  • Kitchen gadgets and small housewares
  • Small furniture
  • Lamps and lighting
  • Music equipment and instruments
  • Home décor
  • Costume jewelry
  • Books
  • Office and school supplies
  • Hand tools
  • Art and craft supplies
  • Bins, baskets, and containers
  • Toys and games
  • Pet supplies

What we can't take

These are either unsafe for us to handle, impossible to store, or better served by another organization.

  • Clothing and shoes
  • Large appliances
  • Fine jewelry
  • Medications
  • Firearms and ammunition
  • Live plants or animals
  • Power tools
  • Automotive parts, tires, fluids

Not sure about an item? Email office@northshorepres.org before you load the car.
