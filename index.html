Have a look before we build it for real

A preview of the new North Shore Presbyterian website

Every page is here and everything works — click the menus, follow the links, try it on your phone. Nothing is live yet, and nothing you do can break anything.

  1. Click any page below to open it, then use the menu across the top to move around.
  2. When something strikes you — good or bad — press the Leave feedback button in the bottom-right corner, type a note, and press Save note.
  3. When you've finished, press Copy all notes on any page. That gathers everything you wrote across every page. Paste it into an email and send it back.

Shared Kitchen — the page that is already live

Not part of this preview. It is a separate piece of work, in the Shared Kitchen deck’s own branding, and it is already published on the real site at northshorepres.org/shared-kitchen. It sits here so you can see the two side by side — it deliberately does not use the design above.

Two things worth knowing while you look.

The photographs are stand-ins. We have only one real photograph of the congregation — the one on the front page — so every other picture is a placeholder from a free photo library. They show where photographs go, not which photographs. Getting real ones taken would change this site more than anything else on the list.

A few pages are marked needs your words. Those are waiting on information nobody has written down anywhere yet, so please ignore the wording there and instead tell us what ought to be on them.
