Building on Our Legacy

For more than 100 years, North Shore Presbyterian Church has served as a place of welcome, faith, and community. This project continues that legacy — creating a welcoming space for hospitality and spiritual bonding as we nourish the mind, body, and spirit of our neighbors.

Ultimately, the shared kitchen serves as an extension of our communion table, allowing us to be "good neighbors" through meaningful partnerships that embody a "Loaves and Fishes" mission.

Being Good Stewards

The Shared Kitchen is a unique initiative to transform an underutilized area of our building into a thriving community hub while expanding our long-standing food ministry.

By partnering with local food entrepreneurs and community groups, we are thoughtfully stewarding our building and financial resources to increase the church's viability and visibility in the neighborhood.

Enlivens the Building

This project provides bridges within groups that use the church space for other activities, strengthening the church's presence in the neighborhood and creating new opportunities for service, connection, and outreach.

Our shared kitchen also creates a center for daily activity — after-school programs, intergenerational cooking classes, community meals, and more.

"More than a kitchen, this space is a gathering place — where neighbors connect, learn, celebrate, and care for one another."

A Place for Community

Our vision is for the Shared Kitchen to become a center of daily life and activity — a place where people of all ages and backgrounds gather, learn, and grow together.

  • After-school programs & intergenerational cooking classes
  • Community meals and outreach events
  • Fundraisers, family reunions, and baby showers
  • Nonprofit gatherings and educational workshops

Together, we can make a difference.

As this ministry grows, we invite you to be part of the story — through volunteering, participating in events, partnering with us, or simply sharing a meal. Join us for worship on Sundays and follow our journey on social media.
