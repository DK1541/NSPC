Staff

Rev. Jeannine Oakes Daugherty

Pastor

Jeannine has been an ordained teaching elder in the PC(USA) since May 2015. She studied film and photography at UIC, completed her divinity studies at Yale, and holds an MA in Urban Ministry from McCormick Theological Seminary. She moved to Wisconsin in January 2024 with her wife Denise and their three daughters.

jeannine@northshorepres.org

Session

The Session is the elected board of elders responsible for the spiritual life, mission, and governance of North Shore Presbyterian Church. Elders serve three-year terms and lead alongside the pastor.

Get in touch with the Session

To bring something to the Session's attention, contact the church office at office@northshorepres.org.

Deacons

Our Deacons carry out the congregation's ministry of care — visiting members, supporting families through illness and loss, and serving as "deacon buddies" to the children of the church throughout the year.

Need care or know someone who does?

Reach the Deacons through the church office at office@northshorepres.org.
