The people who keep this place open

A pastor, two musicians, an organist, and an office that has held a century of records. Between them they lead worship, teach the children, and answer the phone when you call.

Staff

JD

Rev. Jeannine Oakes Daugherty

Pastor

Ordained a teaching elder in the PC(USA) since May 2015. She studied film and photography at UIC, completed her divinity studies at Yale, and holds an MA in Urban Ministry from McCormick Theological Seminary. She moved to Wisconsin in January 2024 with her wife Denise and their three daughters.

jeannine@northshorepres.org

SD

Sean DeBoth

Director of Music

Director of Music at North Shore Presbyterian since 2004. He teaches at Lake Denoon Middle School and holds degrees from Carroll College and UWM. He lives in Mukwonago with his wife and three children.

nspcmusic@gmail.com

JP

Jeff Peterson

Organist

Professor emeritus at UWM, with degrees from the Oberlin College Conservatory of Music and the University of Illinois. He has played for churches for more than four decades, and spends his summers boating and fishing at the family cottage up north.

jeffry@uwm.edu

LH

Office & Financial Assistant

office@northshorepres.org

What we can take

Gently used and in working order — if you'd hand it to a friend, we'd love to have it.

  • Kitchen gadgets
  • Small furniture
  • Lamps
  • Music equipment
  • Home décor
  • Costume jewelry
  • Books
  • Office supplies
  • Hand tools
  • Art supplies
  • Bins and baskets
  • Toys and games
  • Pet supplies

What we can't

Unsafe to handle, impossible to store, or better served somewhere else.

  • Clothing and shoes
  • Large appliances
  • Fine jewelry
  • Medications
  • Firearms
  • Live plants or animals
  • Power tools
  • Automotive items
