New here?

Three things people ask before their first Sunday

What do I wear?

Whatever you own. You'll see suits and you'll see jeans in the same pew.

What about my kids?

Bring them. There's Godly Play for the little ones, Sunday School for grades 1–5, and nobody minds noise.

Do I have to believe it all?

No. This congregation holds a wide range of politics and theology. You are welcome wherever you are.

Out in the neighborhood

Most of what we do happens outside the building

A summer camp, an after-school program, a free store, and a block party that closes the street.

Compassion Camp

Summer day camp for children entering 4K through 5th grade, built on kindness and hands-on service.

Good Neighbour Exchange

Two donation days, then a free store day. Everything costs nothing and nobody is asked why they came.

jeannine@northshorepres.org
